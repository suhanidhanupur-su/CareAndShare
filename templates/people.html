Leadership, Governance & Social Action

Care & Share Society, Sardhana is driven by a passionate collective of social workers, philanthropists, and community leaders who dedicate their time, expertise, and resources to uplift underprivileged families. Our leadership team ensures that every initiative in healthcare, education, and community welfare is carried out with transparency, compassion, and meaningful impact.

A Message from Ainuddin Shah Ji

"We strongly believe that every individual deserves an opportunity to live a healthy, dignified, and fulfilling life. We are all bound by our duty to society and to our fellow citizens. We should commit ourselves to helping the less fortunate and the needy by sharing our resources with those who are deprived."

"This is the right time to come forward and support this noble cause of care and share. Every contribution and helping hand of yours will be fully utilized to uplift communities and bring hope to families in need."

"Serving humanity is our highest moral duty. Lack of resources should never deny a child an education or a patient life-saving healthcare."
