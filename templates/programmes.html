Flagship Healthcare
PAEDIATRIC CARDIOLOGY Sardhana & Beyond

Hridayasparsham (Pro Heart)

Hridayasparsham is the first flagship initiative of Care and Share Society. Guided by Senior Social Worker Ainuddin Shah Ji, this programme focuses on paediatric cardiology, facilitating life-saving heart surgeries and essential medical treatments for children from economically disadvantaged families.

  • Life-saving heart surgery support for underprivileged infants and children
  • Partnering with reputed cardiac healthcare institutions
  • Post-operative care and rehabilitation monitoring for families

SOCIAL AWARENESS Sardhana Region

Vazhikatti (Guide • Anti-Drug Campaign)

Vazhikatti is an intensive social awareness campaign and mentorship movement against substance abuse and social vices. Initiated to protect the youth, this campaign provides positive counseling, educational direction, and community solidarity under Ainuddin Shah Ji's guidance.

  • Youth seminars and educational workshops in local schools and neighborhoods
  • Community counseling and rehabilitation guidance for vulnerable youth
  • Mobilizing young volunteers for constructive social welfare projects

Educational Aid
STUDENT WELFARE Sardhana Schools

Vidhya Amruthu (Educational Aid & Kits)

Vidhya Amruthu is the society's core educational venture dedicated to the belief that lack of money must never deprive a deserving child of quality schooling. The programme distributes study kits, textbooks, uniforms, and financial assistance to underprivileged students.

  • Distribution of complete academic study kits, notebooks, and learning supplies
  • School fees and examination support for economically disadvantaged students
  • Merit encouragement and scholarship drives across rural Sardhana

SPECIALIZED HEALTHCARE Medical Outreach

Kathod Kathoram (Hearing Care & Speech Support)

Kathod Kathoram is a dedicated humanitarian medical initiative striving to restore hope and communication for individuals affected by hearing impairment, providing screening camps and assistance for speech therapy and specialized care.

  • Free audiometric screenings and early hearing loss identification
  • Facilitating medical guidance for corrective procedures and devices
  • Support for children needing specialized speech therapy

Child Health
PAEDIATRIC SURGERY Healthcare Desk

Valsalyam (Paediatric Surgical Care)

Valsalyam is a compassionate child healthcare programme providing medical support, pediatric surgical assistance, and rehabilitative care for underprivileged children under the age of 14 facing complex health challenges.

  • Facilitating access to advanced pediatric surgeries and treatments
  • Complete support for destitute families during hospitalization
  • Pre-operative nutritional counseling and medical assistance

Critical Medical Relief
CRITICAL AID Patient Assistance

Sukrutham (Critical Medical Relief)

Sukrutham provides humanitarian solidarity and medical aid to vulnerable individuals facing catastrophic health crises such as kidney failure, prolonged intensive care, and acute emergencies where medical costs exceed household means.

  • Financial guidance and aid for prolonged medical treatments and dialysis
  • Medical camp outreach and compassionate family counseling
  • Connecting patients with essential pharmaceutical aid and diagnostic centers
Guidance: Ainuddin Shah Ji Apply for Aid
Vision For All
COMMUNITY EYE CARE Rural Camps

Kaazhcha (Community Vision Camps)

Kaazhcha conducts free comprehensive eye checkup camps across Sardhana and neighboring villages. The initiative focuses on early vision diagnosis, free spectacles distribution, and cataract identification for senior citizens and students.

  • Free vision screening and eye refraction tests by optometrists
  • Free distribution of customized corrective spectacles to the needy
  • Cataract identification and guidance for charitable surgery support
Guidance: Ainuddin Shah Ji Organize A Camp

SOCIAL WELFARE Sardhana District

Community Welfare & Seasonal Relief

Direct grassroots assistance for destitute households across Sardhana. Our volunteer network coordinates seasonal winter blanket drives, emergency grocery kits, and relief distribution under the supervision of Ainuddin Shah Ji.

  • Winter warmth drives delivering warm blankets and woolens to the poor
  • Essential food grain and grocery kits for destitute elderly individuals
  • Emergency relief and immediate social support during seasonal hardship
