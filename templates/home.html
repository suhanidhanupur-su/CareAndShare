CARE & SHARE SOCIETY, SARDHANA

Care & Share Society, Sardhana is a non-profit organization dedicated to serving humanity and supporting the welfare of the community. Under the guidance and leadership of our respected senior social worker, Ainuddine Shah Ji, the society strives to uplift the lives of underprivileged and less fortunate individuals through various healthcare, educational, social welfare, and community development initiatives.

Dear Friends & Well-Wishers,

We strongly believe that every individual deserves an opportunity to live a healthy, dignified, and fulfilling life. We are all bound by our duty to society and to our fellow citizens. We should commit ourselves to helping the less fortunate and the needy by sharing our resources with those who are deprived. This is the right time to come forward and support this noble cause of care and share.
