CARE & SHARE SOCIETY, SARDHANA

Care & Share Society, Sardhana is a non-profit organization dedicated to serving humanity and supporting the welfare of the community. Under the guidance and leadership of our respected senior social worker, Ainuddin Shah Ji, the society strives to uplift the lives of underprivileged and less fortunate individuals through various healthcare, educational, social welfare, and community development initiatives.

We strongly believe that every individual deserves an opportunity to live a healthy, dignified, and fulfilling life. Through the spirit of care, sharing, compassion, and collective responsibility, we aim to create a positive and meaningful difference in society.

Our Initiatives

We strive to uplift the lives of the less fortunate through various healthcare and educational programmes.

Hridayasparsham (Pro Heart)

Hridayasparsham is the first flagship initiative of Care and Share Foundation. The programme focuses on paediatric cardiology and life-saving heart surgeries for children.

Leadership: Ainuddin Shah Ji

Vazhikatti (Guide)

Care and Share Foundation announced an intensive awareness campaign against drugs and substance abuse called Vazhikatti to guide the youth toward a better tomorrow.

Vidhya Amruthu
