ABOUT US

Care & Share Society, Sardhana

Care & Share Society, Sardhana is a non-profit organization dedicated to serving humanity and supporting the welfare of the community. Under the guidance and leadership of our respected senior social worker, Ainuddine Shah Ji, the society strives to uplift the lives of underprivileged and less fortunate individuals through various healthcare, educational, social welfare, and community development initiatives.

We strongly believe that every individual deserves an opportunity to live a healthy, dignified, and fulfilling life. Through the spirit of care, sharing, compassion, and collective responsibility, we aim to create a positive and meaningful difference in society.

LEADERSHIP & GUIDANCE

Mr. Ainuddin Shah

Senior Social Worker • Care & Share Society

"Serving humanity is our highest moral duty. Through Care & Share Society Sardhana, we have resolved to reach the poorest and most vulnerable, ensuring that lack of resources never denies a child an education or a patient life-saving healthcare."

Under his compassionate vision, our volunteers actively run relief camps, educational support drives, and healthcare outreach programmes throughout the region.

Our Vision

To build a society free from the hardships of poverty and ill health, where every individual has access to better opportunities and a quality life. We aspire to bridge the gap between those who have resources in abundance and those who are deprived, promoting equality, dignity, and social well-being.

Our Mission

Our mission is to share resources and opportunities from those who have them in surplus with those who are in need. Through meaningful initiatives in healthcare, education, and social welfare, we strive to support the underprivileged, empower communities, and help create a better and more compassionate society for all.

Our Emblem & Philosophy

"Together We Care, Together We Share"

The Care & Share Society Sardhana emblem embodies the sacred bond between those who give and those in need. The protective hands symbolize sheltering the vulnerable, while the open book and growing sapling represent our commitment to empowering youth through education and community health. Together, we work towards a society grounded in compassion, mutual support, and dignity for all.
